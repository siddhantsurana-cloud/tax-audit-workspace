Universal Excel Auto-Feed

6

Zero-Download Cloud Sync

Click "☁️ Transmit Workings to CA" to securely push all prepared tables directly to Bihani Rashmi & Co.'s audit repository with zero popup downloads. The CA audit team in any city clicks "🔄 Fetch Remote Submission" to pull live data.
